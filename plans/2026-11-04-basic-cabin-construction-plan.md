# Basic Norwegian Mountain Cabin Construction Plan

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** Not started  
**Next stage:** Confirm layout and requirements.

## Goal

Build a simple, modern Norwegian mountain cabin with 3 bedrooms, 1 kitchen, 2 bathrooms, and 1 living room. Use timber framing, wood cladding, and large insulated glass windows for mountain views. Do not use bricks.

## Stages

1. **Not started:** Confirm the layout, materials, site conditions, and budget. Obtain professional designs for local snow, wind, and cold, plus required approvals.
2. **Not started:** Build the approved foundation, timber walls, roof, and insulated glazing. Insulate and weatherproof the shell. Install plumbing, electrical services, ventilation, heating, and room finishes. Complete required checks before covering work.
3. **Not started:** Complete required inspections, test services, fix defects, and hand over the cabin.

## Done when

All required rooms are complete, wood and glass match the approved design, no bricks are used, the shell is insulated and weatherproof, services work, required inspections and approvals are complete, and the cabin is ready for handover.
