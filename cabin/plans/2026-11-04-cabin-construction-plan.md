# Simple Norwegian Mountain Cabin Construction Plan

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** Planning complete; construction has not started.  
**Next stage:** Confirm layout and project scope.

## Goal

Plan a modern Norwegian mountain cabin for presentation. It will include **3 bedrooms, 1 kitchen, 2 bathrooms, and 1 living room**. **Use wood and glass. Do not use bricks.**

For this example, assume a simple single-storey cabin with timber framing, wood cladding, and large insulated glass windows for mountain views. Single-storey construction is an illustrative assumption, not a confirmed site or engineering requirement. This plan is not a construction-ready design.

## Sequence

The main sequence is **1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7**. Each stage must pass its completion check before dependent work begins. Design work and trade scheduling may overlap when coordinated; inspections must happen before relevant work is concealed.
