# Modern Norwegian Mountain Cabin

The cabin must have:

- 3 bedrooms
- 1 kitchen
- 2 bathrooms
- 1 living room

Keep the design simple and contemporary, suited to a Norwegian mountain setting.
Use timber framing and wood cladding with large insulated glass windows for
mountain views. Do not use bricks.

Provide a well-insulated, weatherproof shell and heating for cold conditions.
Qualified local professionals must design the foundation, structure, roof, and
glazing for the site's snow loads, wind, and local requirements.
